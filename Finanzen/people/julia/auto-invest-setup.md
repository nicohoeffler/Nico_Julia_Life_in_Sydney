## 3. Step by step

### A. Account (once)
1. **Open the account** in the Betashares Direct app or website: Australian tax resident, 18+, Australian residential address, ID (driver licence or passport). Enter her **Tax File Number** during sign-up. Without a TFN, tax is withheld from distributions at the top rate.
2. **Note the Cash Wallet details** shown in the app (BSB and account number in her name).

### B. Tomorrow: first month by hand
3. **Move $1,300** from ANZ to the Cash Wallet (Osko/PayID if offered, otherwise a normal transfer, which can take 1–2 business days). Buying tomorrow only works once the account is verified and the money shows in the wallet.
4. **Buy BGBL $1,144 and BEMG $156.** Avoid the first 30 minutes after the 10:00 open, when ETF spreads are usually widest. A good window is **10:30–15:30 Sydney time**. How Betashares Direct executes one-off and fractional orders is not verified, so check the order screen.

### C. From December: auto-invest on the 19th
5. **ANZ recurring transfer:** $1,300 to the Wallet BSB/account, monthly on the **17th**, first payment 17 Dec 2026.
6. **Auto-invest plan:** BGBL $1,144 + BEMG $156, monthly, start **19 Dec 2026** (if that is a weekend, check in the app how the date shifts).
7. **Distributions:** set BGBL distributions to **reinvest automatically** (allowed under the Betashares Direct PDS of 9 Sep 2026, section 2.5; exact menu name not verified). BEMG does not distribute.
8. **Calendar reminder for mid-September 2027:** after the 19 Aug 2027 purchase, change the ANZ transfer to $300 and the auto-invest to $264 / $36.

## 4. Things to know

- **Market just dropped?** That doesn't change the plan. Nobody can reliably time the bottom. Staggering means she keeps buying if prices fall further. Historically, investing a lump sum all at once wins in about 2 out of 3 cases. Staggering is insurance against regret, not a return booster.
- **Why the 19th?** It's fine, but not because it is a "best day". We tested 12–30 years of data: no day of the month is reliably better, and differences are around 0.1 % (see [kauftag-analyse](../../docs/research/2026-11-04_kauftag-analyse.md)). What matters is that the transfer arrives before the buy date.
- **Tax:** reinvested distributions are still taxable in the year they are paid. Betashares sends an annual tax statement (AMMA). Keep it for the tax return.
- **Selling:** possible on any ASX trading day in the app. The money arrives in the Cash Wallet after settlement (T+2) and can be withdrawn to ANZ. See the Safety Net page.
- **Pause:** auto-invest can be paused or changed at any time in the app.
