Yes, you can always sell. Here's why you usually shouldn't.

Your ETF money is never locked away

Think of it like this. Your investments are a garden, not a safe. You can pick the fruit any day you like. But if you pull out the plants in winter, they never get to grow back.

Can I get my money out?

Selling takes a few taps and a few days

  1. 1Open the Betashares Direct app on any ASX trading day and tap Sell.
  2. 2The sale settles in 2 business days.
  3. 3The cash lands in your Betashares wallet.
  4. 4Transfer it to your ANZ account. It usually arrives within a few days.

BGBL and BEMG trade on the ASX every trading day, so there is always a buyer. One thing to know: selling within 12 months of buying means paying full tax on the gain. After 12 months, only half the gain is taxed.

So why not sell when prices fall?

Because falling prices are when monthly saving works hardest

Every month you put in the same $1,000. When the price is low, that $1,000 buys you more pieces of the world. Tap a month below to see what happens if you sell then.

The honest part

Markets do fall, sometimes a lot

Global shares have fallen by a third or more several times in history, and getting back up has sometimes taken years. It will happen while you own your ETFs. That's normal. A loss only becomes real if you sell during the dip. That's why the next part matters.

Saving with a safety net

Three pots, so you never have to sell in a dip

Money you might need soon never goes into the investment pot. Then a dip can't force your hand.

Safety net
≈ $10,000

About 6 months of living costs, in an ANZ savings account. This is the money you grab for an emergency. Refill it afterwards.

Tax pot
≈ $3,000–5,000

Set aside for tax on your business income, so tax time is never a surprise.

Growth pot
$10,000 + monthly

Invested $1,000 a month over 10 months, plus a small monthly amount. Money for 5+ years from now.

Pot sizes are from our first conversation on 3 November 2026. We'll adjust them once we've looked at your real monthly spending.

Your three rules
  • 1Only invest money you won't need for at least 5 years.
  • 2When prices drop, do nothing. Your monthly auto-invest keeps buying cheaper.
  • 3Need cash? Take it from the safety net first. The growth pot is for future you.
